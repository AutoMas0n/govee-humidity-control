## Why

Previous attempts to control the H5080 smart plug over BLE (documented in `h5080-ble-protocol`) all failed: standard XOR-checksum frames, AWS-value variants, and the V2 encrypted handshake all produced no relay toggle despite successful BLE writes. The Govee app proves BLE control is possible (plug toggles with WiFi off), but the actual protocol is unknown.

Instead of implementing another speculative protocol, this change captures the real H5080 BLE traffic generated by the Govee app. This is the only reliable way to determine the exact command format, characteristic usage, encryption (if any), and pairing sequence required.

## What Changes

- **New**: BLE packet capture script (`btmon` / `hcidump`) on the Raspberry Pi that records all H5080 traffic while the Govee app toggles the plug
- **New**: Parse and document the captured packets to extract the real protocol format
- **New**: Replay the captured commands to confirm they toggle the plug
- **Supersedes**: The `h5080-ble-protocol` change is marked as `FAILED-ATTEMPT` and archived
